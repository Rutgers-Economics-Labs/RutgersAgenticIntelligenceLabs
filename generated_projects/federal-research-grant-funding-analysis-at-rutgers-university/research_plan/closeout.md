# Closeout

Status: locally validation-ready

The federal grants project now functions as a proper local RAIL project. It has deterministic scripts, public-source caches, processed data, final artifacts, claim/source evidence links, artifact lineage, and a project verifier that is run by the rail local validation command.

Important caveat: the project is a public-data federal award analysis, not an audited internal Rutgers department ranking. The department-level deliverable is intentionally framed as a limitation and next-step crosswalk requirement because the public award feeds do not reliably encode Rutgers academic departments.
