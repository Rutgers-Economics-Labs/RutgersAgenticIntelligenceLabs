# Reviewer 2 Report

## Decision

Revise before any causal or department-ranking claim; acceptable as a descriptive public-data concentration report.

## Major Concerns

- The public feeds do not contain a Rutgers department crosswalk, so the report correctly avoids department-level causal or performance rankings.
- The dollar totals are exposure-style public award records, not an audited internal sponsored-research ledger.
- Continuing pre-window awards dominate observed dollars, so the analysis cannot be interpreted as new FY2021-FY2025 funding growth.

## Required Scope Label

This project should close only as `descriptive`. A causal upgrade would require peer universities, pre/post policy or agency-shock timing, a matched comparison design, standard errors, pre-trend diagnostics, and robustness checks.
